  1. The Broadsheet
    Fraunces + EB Garamond · serif editorial

    A literary periodical. Outline circles, footnoted region markers (i, ii, iii…), conferences laid out as a three-column reference list below. Cream paper or midnight ink, single warm-red accent. The most "thing you'd read" of the five.

    Strict grid, hairline circles, generous whitespace, big variable-weight numerals showing each region's count. A single chartreuse accent calls out the centre. The "shipped product" feel — restrained, confident, fast to scan.
